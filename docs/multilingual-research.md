# Multilingual research

English and Chinese original passages are retained. Existing lexical English tokens and Chinese character/bigrams are reused; this is not semantic cross-language retrieval. Reviews may add translations and normalized facts without replacing originals. Fidelity is a human responsibility and currently unmeasured.

Entity aliases and terminology mappings are planned. Do not assume organizational/vehicle aliases automatically. Future mappings require provenance, ambiguity and review status. No translator or LLM is configured.
